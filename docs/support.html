Support

Support is through GitHub Issues. A free GitHub account is needed to post.

Report a problem

Open an issue and include:

  1. the prompt you typed,
  2. the image (only if it shows no one who hasn't agreed to it),
  3. what's wrong: face or likeness, skin, eyes or pointing, room layout, stereotype, looks AI-made, text in the image, or other.

Issues are public. Don't post personal details or private photos. Most issues get a reply within a few days.

Quick fixes

  • Plugin not used: start a new chat or Codex session after installing, and mention what you want in the image.
  • Want your exact wording: say "use my prompt exactly".
  • Something in the image is off: say what's wrong ("she should look at the man on the left") and ask for a fix.
  • Remove it: turn the plugin off in ChatGPT or Codex settings, or run codex plugin remove kenya-real-images@pichalabs.

Known limits

  • Screens and whiteboards can still show words the image model made up.
  • A presenter's gaze sometimes lands between listeners instead of on one person.
  • With your own photos, the likeness varies from image to image.

Other questions

Privacy, licensing or anything else: open an issue. See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
