Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to Kenya Real Images version 1.0.0 and later, published by PichaLabs.

Summary

Kenya Real Images collects no personal data. It is a set of written instructions (a "skill") that runs inside ChatGPT or Codex. It has no server, no account, no analytics, no tracking and no connection to any service run by PichaLabs. PichaLabs never receives your prompts, your photos or your images.

What data is handled, and by whom

  • Your prompts and messages. When you ask for an image, ChatGPT or Codex reads your request together with the plugin's instructions and generates the image. This happens entirely within OpenAI's products.
  • Photos you upload. If you attach photos of yourself or someone else, they are processed by ChatGPT or Codex (OpenAI) to make the image. PichaLabs does not receive, see or store them.
  • Generated images. Images are created and stored by ChatGPT or Codex, or saved to a folder on your own computer when you use Codex locally. PichaLabs does not receive them.

How OpenAI handles this data, including how long it is kept and whether it is used to improve models, is set by OpenAI's own privacy policy and your ChatGPT settings: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Purposes

The plugin's only purpose is to help ChatGPT and Codex make realistic images of Kenyan people and places that you ask for. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or sale.

Recipients

The plugin itself sends data to no one. Your content is shared only with OpenAI, as part of your normal use of ChatGPT or Codex.

Retention

PichaLabs keeps nothing, because it receives nothing. Retention of your chats, uploads and images is controlled by OpenAI and by you (for example by deleting chats or turning off chat history in ChatGPT).

Your controls

  • Turn the plugin off or remove it at any time in ChatGPT or Codex.
  • Delete chats, uploaded files and images in ChatGPT, and manage data controls in your ChatGPT settings.
  • Only upload photos of people who have agreed to it.

Support requests

If you choose to report a problem on GitHub Issues, what you post there (text, prompts, images) is public and handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement. Do not post personal information or photos of people who have not agreed. You can edit or delete your own issue comments, or ask us to remove an issue.

Children

The plugin is not directed at children under 13. Use by teenagers is subject to OpenAI's age requirements for ChatGPT and Codex.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Past versions are kept in the project history.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/pichalabs/kenya-real-images/issues. See Support.
