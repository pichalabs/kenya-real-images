## Nairobi

- **CBD:** Kenyatta Avenue, Moi Avenue, Tom Mboya Street crowds, the Hilton and KICC area, office workers, hawkers, shoe shiners, matatu stages at Ambassadeur and Odeon, Archives.
- **Business and upmarket:** Westlands and Upper Hill office towers, Kilimani and Kileleshwa apartments, Karen and Runda houses with gardens and gates, Gigiri, malls such as Sarit, Two Rivers and the Junction (no readable signs), co-working spaces, rooftop cafes.
- **Estates and neighbourhoods:** Eastlands estates (Buruburu, Umoja, Donholm, Kayole) with painted gate walls and shops below flats, South B and South C, Rongai and Kitengela commuter towns, Eastleigh's busy Somali-run malls, Parklands with Kenyan Indian homes, temples and shops, Kibera and Mathare when the story calls for it, shown with dignity.
- **Markets:** Gikomba (second-hand clothes, mitumba), Marikiti, Toi, Maasai Market on its rotating days, Wakulima.
- **Everyday sights:** matatus with loud colours and tinted windows, boda bodas with passengers in reflective vests, mama mboga stalls, kiosks with a serving window, M-Pesa agent booths, chips and smokie vendors, roast maize sellers, jacaranda trees in October-November, cold grey July mornings, heavy rain in April-May and November.

## Other towns and cities

- **Mombasa:** Old Town alleys with carved Swahili doors, Fort Jesus walls, Likoni ferry crowds, tuk-tuks, Nyali and Bamburi beaches, mahamri and chai, buibui and kanzu, Kenyans of Swahili, Arab and Indian heritage.
- **Kisumu:** Lake Victoria shoreline at Dunga, fish markets with tilapia and omena, Kisumu's wide new roads and port.
- **Nakuru, Naivasha, Eldoret, Kitale:** Rift Valley towns with wide streets, flower farms and greenhouses near Naivasha, maize and wheat farms around Eldoret and Kitale, athletes training on red-earth roads in Iten.
- **Central Kenya (Nyeri, Murang'a, Kiambu, Thika, Meru, Embu):** tea and coffee farms on green hills, dairy cows, cooperative stores, Thika's hardware and industrial areas, Mt Kenya on clear days.
- **Western Kenya (Kakamega, Bungoma, Kisii):** green hills, sugarcane, Kakamega forest edges, busy market days, soapstone carving in Kisii.
- **Eastern Kenya (Machakos, Kitui, Makueni):** dry red hills, mango and fruit farms, Machakos' busy town centre.
- **North and northeast (Garissa, Isiolo, Marsabit, Lodwar, Wajir):** hot dry light, Somali, Borana, Turkana and Rendille communities, livestock markets, modern towns with mosques and solar panels.
- **Coast beyond Mombasa:** Diani, Watamu, Malindi and Kilifi beaches, Lamu's donkeys and dhows, Mijikenda homesteads, coconut and cashew trees.

## Settings by purpose

- **Work:** hotel conference rooms with round tables, county offices with old wooden furniture, co-working spaces, open-plan offices, factories in Athi River and Industrial Area, warehouses in Embakasi, hospitals and clinics, pharmacies, banks and SACCOs, salons and barbershops, garages (jua kali workshops).
- **Education:** public university lecture halls, campus lawns, school classrooms with wooden desks in rows, school buses, libraries, ECDE classrooms with colourful walls.
- **Home:** sitting rooms with a sofa set, a coffee table and a TV, kitchens with a gas cooker and sufuria, dining tables with chapati, githeri, ugali, sukuma wiki and stew, balconies with laundry, gated compounds.
- **Faith and community:** church services with choirs and plastic chairs, harambees, weddings and ruracio, funerals with tents, mosques at prayer time, community halls.
- **Leisure:** Uhuru Park and Karura forest, nyama choma joints, rooftop lounges, football on a dusty pitch, Sunday family lunch, road trips with a view of the Rift Valley.

## Food

Chapati, mandazi, mahamri, githeri, ugali with sukuma wiki, nyama choma with kachumbari, pilau, samosas, chips mwitu, smokies with kachumbari, mutura, omena and tilapia, chai in a thermos or a plastic mug, mursik in Rift Valley homes, madafu (coconuts) at the coast.
