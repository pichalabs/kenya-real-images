# Kenyan places and details

Pick 1-2 details that fit. Never pile up landmarks or "Kenyan" symbols. Shop names and matatu art stay soft unless the user wants words.

- **Nairobi CBD:** Kenyatta and Moi Avenue, Tom Mboya Street crowds, KICC area, matatu stages at Ambassadeur and Odeon, hawkers, shoe shiners.
- **Upmarket and business:** Westlands and Upper Hill towers, Kilimani and Kileleshwa flats, Karen and Runda gated houses, malls with no readable signs, co-working spaces, rooftop cafes.
- **Estates:** Buruburu, Umoja, Donholm, Kayole with painted gate walls and shops below flats; South B and C; Rongai, Kitengela; Eastleigh's Somali-run malls; Parklands with Kenyan Indian homes and temples; Kibera and Mathare when the story calls for it, with dignity.
- **Markets:** Gikomba mitumba, Marikiti, Toi, Wakulima, Maasai Market.
- **Everyday sights:** matatus with loud colours, boda bodas with riders in reflective vests, mama mboga stalls, kiosks, M-Pesa booths, smokie and roast maize vendors, jacaranda in Oct-Nov, cold grey July mornings, rain in Apr-May and Nov.
- **Mombasa and coast:** Old Town carved doors, Likoni ferry, tuk-tuks, Nyali and Diani beaches, Lamu dhows and donkeys, buibui and kanzu, Swahili, Arab and Indian heritage.
- **Kisumu:** Lake Victoria at Dunga, tilapia and omena markets.
- **Rift Valley:** Nakuru and Eldoret wide streets, Naivasha flower greenhouses, maize farms around Kitale, runners on red-earth roads in Iten.
- **Central:** tea and coffee on green hills around Nyeri, Murang'a, Kiambu, dairy cows, Thika's hardware area, Mt Kenya on clear days.
- **Western and Eastern:** Kakamega green hills and sugarcane, Kisii soapstone, Machakos and Kitui dry red hills and mango farms.
- **North:** Garissa, Isiolo, Marsabit, Lodwar: hot dry light, livestock markets, mosques, solar panels.
- **By purpose:** hotel conference rooms, county offices, open-plan offices, Industrial Area factories, clinics, pharmacies, SACCOs, salons, jua kali garages; university lecture halls and campus walkways, classrooms with wooden desks; sitting rooms with a sofa set and TV, kitchens with a gas cooker and sufuria; churches with plastic chairs, harambees, ruracio, funeral tents; Karura forest, nyama choma joints, dusty football pitches.
- **Food:** chapati, mandazi, githeri, ugali and sukuma wiki, nyama choma with kachumbari, pilau, samosas, smokies, chai in a thermos.
