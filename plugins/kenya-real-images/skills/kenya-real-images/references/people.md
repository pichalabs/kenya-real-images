# Describing Kenyans as real, varied people

Generic words ("African", "black woman") pull in stock, aid and travel imagery. Brief each main person like a casting director: an individual from a real place.

## Each main person gets

- **Place and age:** "a man in his 30s from Kisumu", "a woman in her 50s who runs a shop in Nyeri", "a 19-year-old student at Kenyatta University". Name a community (Kikuyu, Luo, Luhya, Kalenjin, Kamba, Maasai, Somali, Swahili, Kenyan Indian...) only when it matters, such as a ceremony, never as a shortcut for looks.
- **Skin depth + undertone**, never "dark-skinned" alone. Depth: light brown, caramel, medium brown, chestnut, deep brown, very deep brown. Undertone: warm (golden, red, copper), neutral, cool (plum). In a group, give people different tones.
- **Hair:** short cropped, fade with a line-up, teeny afro, full afro, twist-out, finger coils, puff, cornrows, box or knotless braids, locs, African threading, relaxed bob, silk press, wig, dhuku, hijab, kofia, cap. Vary it: not every woman has braids, not every man a fade.
- **One or two face details:** round or long face, high cheekbones, full cheeks, dimples, a gap in the front teeth, laugh lines, wide-set eyes, glasses, a broad or narrow nose.
- **Build in plain words:** slim, average, stocky, tall and lanky, short and broad, plus-size, older and slightly stooped. Bodies have natural proportions; for young women in fitted clothes, say "natural, average figure" so the model does not exaggerate it.
- **Clothes that fit the place, weather and moment:** office shirts, jeans, hoodies, sweaters on cold Nairobi mornings, school uniforms, overalls, reflective vests on boda riders, gumboots on farms. Kitenge, kanga and ankara on some people, not everyone. Occasion dress (kanzu, buibui, Maasai shuka and beadwork, ruracio outfits, choir robes) only when the occasion calls for it.

Use "ordinary looks, not a model" when faces or bodies drift glossy.

## Skin line (every photo with people)

Correct exposure for every skin tone in the frame, natural undertones and real texture with visible pores, no grey, ashy or orange cast, no lightening, no plastic shine, no crushed shadows; deep skin keeps its detail and warmth.

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| black African, Kenyan person, African people | town plus individual description |
| tribal, native, exotic, primitive, ebony, warrior | the named community and occasion, or nothing |
| village as default, poor, struggling, rural Africa | the actual town, farm or estate and the real action |
| beautiful Kenyan woman | an individual description and a real action |
| African print on everyone | specific clothing per person |
| safari, savannah sunset, acacia silhouette | the place where the story happens |
| traditional (unnamed) | the named garment and occasion, or modern clothes |

## Groups

Describe 2-3 people individually, then: "the others vary in age, build, skin tone and hair, as you would expect in [place], going about their own business".
