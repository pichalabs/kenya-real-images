# Space: gaze, pointing, bodies and layout

Images feel AI-made at a glance when the space does not add up: eyes that slide past each other, a cook standing inside the wall, a table top with nothing under it. The model is told what people do but not where everything is. Place people and objects first, then say who looks at what.

## Gaze that lands

1. **Position first:** "The trainer stands on the left, facing right. The participant sits on the right, her chair turned toward him, two metres away."
2. **Head, shoulders and eyes to one target:** "her head, shoulders and eyes turn toward him; she looks straight at his face."
3. **Both ends of every eye line:** if A looks at B, say what B looks at; if back, "they make eye contact".
4. **Camera from the side for eye contact,** so both faces are in three-quarter profile. Never both faces turned to the camera while "looking at each other".
5. **Downward gazes are safest** (a phone, a book, food, hands at work). Use them for most people; save eye contact for the 1-2 who matter, framed waist-up or closer.
6. **Speaker and audience:** name the speaker's position; the audience's chairs and heads face it; give 1-2 listeners another action (writing notes, whispering).
7. **Spectators** (race, match, parade, show): name the one target and its position; every spectator faces it and their heads follow it; show watching with actions (filming on a raised phone, shading eyes, leaning forward). Nobody turns their back to it.
8. **Pointing:** the target is in the frame, the fingertip within a hand's width of it, and both people look at the target.
9. **Held things:** say which way phones, books, screens and cameras face.

## Where people can stand (physical logic)

- **Counters and worktops along a wall:** the person stands on the open room side, facing the counter and the wall, with the counter between them and the wall. Never between the counter and the wall. The camera is in the room behind or beside them, so we see them in profile or three-quarter from behind. To see a cook's face from the front, use a kitchen island or a free-standing table with the room behind the cook.
- **Kitchen island or table:** name its full shape ("a rectangular island about two metres long, its base reaching the floor along its whole length"); the person stands on the far side, both hands on the worktop; the whole island is visible or cut off only by the frame edge.
- **Every surface is supported:** tables have all their legs on the floor, counters have cabinets below to the floor, shelves are fixed to a wall. No top that floats, ends in mid-air or changes length.
- **Stoves, sinks, desks:** the person faces them from the open side, at arm's length.
- **Sitting:** the chair is under the person, pulled up to its table, facing it.
- **Bodies:** natural adult proportions, head size in proportion, both feet on the ground when the full body shows, clothes hanging with real weight and folds.

## Rooms

1. **Name the room shape and layout:** "a rectangular classroom, desks in four straight rows facing the board", "a small sitting room: sofa against the back wall, coffee table in front, two armchairs facing the sofa".
2. **Alignment line (every indoor scene with furniture):** "All furniture is aligned to the room: tables and desks square to the walls, chairs facing their tables, rows straight and evenly spaced, everything standing flat on the floor."
3. **Camera view:** square-on (facing one wall, one vanishing point: halls, classrooms) or corner (two walls: offices, sitting rooms, kitchens). Add "one consistent perspective".
4. **Level camera,** correct scale with distance, and only the furniture the scene needs.

## Outdoors

Name the ground line ("a pavement running from bottom left to back right", "stalls in two rows either side of a path"). Vehicles on the road, parallel to it. Buildings and poles vertical.

## Fix sentences

When an image fails, regenerate with one sentence naming the fix: "she was standing between the counter and the wall; she must stand on the room side with the counter in front of her", "the right end of the island floated; the island's base reaches the floor along its full length", "her eyes look past him; her head and eyes turn fully to his face".
