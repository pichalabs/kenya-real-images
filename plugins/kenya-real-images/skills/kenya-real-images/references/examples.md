# Example prompts

Each example shows how the parts fit together, including layout and gaze. Adapt, don't copy. `[skin rendering line]` and `[... block]` mean the text from people.md and styles.md.

## Two people talking (eye contact done right)

Generate an image that looks like an ordinary unedited smartphone photo a colleague took, not a professional or AI image. Subject: a small business coaching session in a Kilimani cafe. A woman in her 50s who owns a hardware shop in Thika, medium brown skin with warm undertones, short relaxed hair and reading glasses pushed up on her head, in a green cardigan, sits on the left of a small square table facing right. Opposite her on the right sits a coach in his 30s, deep brown skin with cool undertones, a low fade and a short beard, in a navy polo, facing left. Layout: the square table sits square to the wall behind it and to the camera; two cups of chai on it. Gaze: she is mid-sentence, her head, shoulders and eyes turned toward him, looking straight at his face; he looks back at her eyes, listening with a slight nod. They make eye contact. Camera: level, at seated height, from the side of the table, so we see both faces in three-quarter profile facing each other; a sugar bowl blurred in the foreground; other customers behind them looking at their own phones. [skin rendering line] Realism requirements: [candid phone block], horizontal 3:2.

## A speaker and an audience (room layout done right)

Generate an image that looks like an ordinary unedited smartphone photo a participant took, not a professional or AI image. Subject: a Saturday digital skills workshop in a church hall in Nakuru. Layout: a rectangular hall; about forty white plastic chairs in six straight rows, parallel to the front wall, all facing the front, with a central aisle; a small table at the front left with a projector. The trainer, a woman in her 30s with light brown skin, long knotless braids tied back and a kitenge shirt, stands at the front left beside the table, facing the audience, holding a microphone in her right hand and gesturing with her left. Gaze: most of the audience look toward her at the front left; one older man in the third row reads a handout; a young woman in the second row whispers to her neighbour. The trainer looks at a man in the front row who has his hand raised. All furniture is aligned to the room: chairs square to the walls, rows straight and evenly spaced, everything flat on the floor. Camera: level, standing at the back of the hall facing the front wall straight on, one-point perspective, heads of the back row in the foreground. [skin rendering line] Realism requirements: [candid phone block], horizontal 3:2.

## Pointing at something (pointing done right)

Generate an image that looks like an ordinary unedited smartphone photo a colleague took, not a professional or AI image. Subject: a pharmacist in her 40s in Kisumu, deep brown skin, short natural hair and a white coat, shows a young pharmacy assistant, a man in his 20s with chestnut skin and short twists, where to find a medicine on a shelf. Layout: they stand side by side in front of a tall shelf that runs parallel to the back wall, both facing it. Gaze: she points with her right index finger at a white box on the third shelf, her fingertip almost touching it; both of them look at that box. Camera: level, from behind and to the right of them at shoulder height, so we see the shelf, the box and the side of both faces. [skin rendering line] Realism requirements: [candid phone block], vertical 4:5.

## Family dinner at home (room and gaze together)

Generate a professional photograph taken by a skilled photographer on a full-frame camera. Subject: a Sunday family lunch in a Buruburu flat. Layout: a rectangular dining table parallel to the sitting room wall, six chairs, two on each long side and one at each end; a sofa set and a TV unit behind. A grandmother in her 70s with deep brown skin, short grey hair and a headscarf sits at the far end facing the camera; her son in his 40s with medium brown skin and a neat beard sits on the left side; his wife with caramel skin and a silk-pressed bob sits on the right; two children of 7 and 12 sit beside them. Food on the table: chapati, beef stew, sukuma wiki, a jug of juice. Gaze: everyone is laughing and looking at the 7-year-old, who is telling a story with his hands; the grandmother looks at him over her glasses. All furniture is aligned to the room, one consistent perspective. Camera: level, at the near end of the table at seated height, square-on to the table. [skin rendering line] Photography requirements: [professional block], horizontal 3:2.

## Street scene (outdoor layout)

Generate a documentary photograph of a real moment, taken by a photojournalist. Subject: the evening rush at a matatu stage on Tom Mboya Street. Layout: the pavement runs from the bottom left toward the back right; matatus parked in a straight line along the kerb, parallel to the pavement; buildings stand upright. A tout in his 20s with very deep brown skin and short dreadlocks leans out of a matatu door calling for passengers; a woman in her 30s with light brown skin and a hijab, carrying shopping bags, looks up at him; office workers walk past looking at their phones. Gaze: the tout looks at the woman; she looks back at him, deciding. Camera: level, from the opposite pavement at standing height. [skin rendering line] Documentary requirements: [documentary block], horizontal 3:2.

## Children's book illustration (drawn style)

A children's book watercolour illustration: an 8-year-old boy from Kisumu with deep brown skin, a big gap-toothed grin and a short haircut, sitting cross-legged on the floor of his family's sitting room, building a toy boat from a plastic bottle; his older sister, 13, with chestnut skin and cornrows, kneels beside him holding the tape and looking at the boat. The sofa behind them sits square to the wall. Warm, soft colours, gentle texture, friendly, real features, no text. Square 1:1.
