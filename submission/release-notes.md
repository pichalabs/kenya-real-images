# Release notes: 1.0.0

First release in the plugin directory.

- Makes images of Kenyan people and places that look like real photos: varied, specific people in real places, correct skin tones, no stereotypes or charity-poster framing.
- Works on everyday requests even when Kenya isn't mentioned, in English, Swahili or Sheng.
- Rewrites long, keyword-heavy AI prompts into plain briefs to avoid the glossy "AI look".
- Fixes spatial mistakes: eye lines, pointing, furniture and camera position.
- Eight styles: candid phone photo, outfit, professional, documentary, event, sports, studio and illustration.
- Put yourself, or someone who agreed, into a new scene from your own photos.
- Collects no data. Free to use.
