## Short description (max ~60 chars)

Natural, believable images of Kenyan people and places.

## Long description

Image models fall back on stereotypes of African people and make spatial mistakes that feel wrong at a glance: eyes that don't land on the person they look at, pointing that misses its target, and furniture at odd angles to the room. Kenya Real Images describes Kenyans as real, varied individuals anchored to real places, renders every skin tone correctly, lays out people, furniture and camera before writing gaze, and checks and fixes each image. It rewrites long AI-generated prompts into plain briefs so results look like real photos, not glossy AI images. Works for everyday photos, family portraits, weddings, fashion, news, education, business, social media and illustration. It can also put you (or someone who agreed) into a new scene from your own photos. A PichaLabs project.

## Starter prompts

1. A candid photo of two friends talking over chai in a Kilimani cafe.
2. A Sunday family lunch in a Buruburu flat.
3. A trainer speaking to a full church hall in Nakuru.
4. A children's book illustration of a boy in Kisumu building a toy boat.

## Countries

Proposed: **all countries where ChatGPT plugins are available** (broad availability), with Kenya as the primary market. The plugin is useful anywhere people need images of Kenya (diaspora, NGOs, media, brands). Confirm before submitting; the alternative is Kenya only.

## Data and permissions (for the review form)

- Collects no data. No server, no account, no analytics, no network calls of its own.
- Uses only ChatGPT/Codex built-in image generation. Uploaded photos are processed by OpenAI, not by the publisher.
- No authentication, no external apps or connectors.
